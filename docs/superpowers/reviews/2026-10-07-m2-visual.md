## Expected differences
- Traffic lights: drawn in the capture; the real window shows the native ones at (13, 14).
- Shortcuts dialog: lists the real keymap (the reference shows six proposed shortcuts), so it is 461.6 px taller.
- Filtered pane header: has `Open as tab` (spec §5.3; also in the approved replica); it pushes `Find in results`
  100.1 px left (`find-bottom`).
- Fonts: glyph-shape differences are expected (spec §7.2).
