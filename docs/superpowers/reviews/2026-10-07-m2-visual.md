## After M3 (2026-10-07)
The keyboard-shortcuts dialog lists three more shortcuts added in M3 (`Pause / resume live follow` · Space, `Hide
rogg` · ⌘H, `Hide others` · ⌥⌘H), but its measured height is unchanged: `dark-compact--shortcuts` still reports a
`dialog` height delta of +461.6 px (app 820 px vs reference 358.4 px). The dialog frame is capped at the viewport
height minus its margins (`max_h`, 900 − 80 = 820 CSS px) and scrolls its body, and with the real keymap it already
hit that cap in M2, so the three new rows only lengthen the scrolled content. Nothing else in the app captures
changed beyond noise: differing pixels moved by at most 0.02 points against the table above (for example
`dark-compact--shortcuts` 4.12% → 4.14%, `light-compact--shortcuts` 20.71% → 20.69%), the worst geometry deltas are
the same (`find-bottom` −100.1, `token-3` +11.7) and the worst colour probes are unchanged.

## Gate decision (2026-10-07)
Approved by the user after checking the real app, with two changes made before closing the gate:
- **Items 1 and 5 (horizontal scrollbar, ruling R3):** the horizontal thumb is shown only on hover over its own strip
  at the bottom of a pane, while it is dragged, and for 1.2 s after a horizontal scroll; hidden, it is neither drawn
  nor clickable. The vertical thumb stays always visible. The replica goldens therefore match the originally approved
  images again (`just visual-check`: 0.000% on all 9 captures); no re-approval was needed.
- **Focus indicator:** the log panes and the filter band show a 1 px accent line exactly on their perimeter, replacing
  spec §5.1's 2 px ring with a 2 px offset (user's preference).
- **Items 2, 3, 4 and 6:** accepted as they are. **Item 7:** fixed in M3 (the workspace is clippy-clean).

Refreshed app captures (`just visual-app`) after these changes, differing pixels: dark-compact 3.95%,
dark-comfortable 3.86%, light-compact 3.86%, light-comfortable 3.75%; all-tabs 3.11–3.15%; history 3.63–4.78%;
shortcuts 4.03–4.14% (dark) and 20.51–20.68% (light, the taller real-keymap dialog). The drop from 4.2–4.5% comes from
the horizontal thumb no longer being drawn. Geometry deltas are unchanged (`find-bottom` −100.1, `token-3` +11.7).
